# MASTER RESEARCH PLAN: MULTIGALAXIAL LIFE
## A 40-Volume Scientific Research, AI/ML Engineering, and Computational Discovery Programme

### Overview & Operational Definition
**Multigalaxial Life** is a formal computational and astrobiological research framework investigating the probability density, physical-chemical prerequisites, emergence mechanisms, remote observational biosignatures, and mathematical principles of self-organizing living systems across galactic environments.

### B. One-Page Research Purpose and Scope Statement
The **Multigalaxial Life Programme** is dedicated to the systematic, mathematically grounded investigation of astrobiological potential across cosmic environments. Rather than asserting extraterrestrial life as an empirical fact, the programme models the **necessary physical and chemical boundary conditions** under which life can arise, survive, and be detected.

The scope spans twelve orders of magnitude in space and time—from quantum coherence in molecular catalysis ($10^{-9}\text{ m}$) to the large-scale filamentary web of the observable universe ($10^{26}\text{ m}$), and from sub-second chemical kinetics to cosmological stellar formation history ($13.8\text{ Gyr}$).

The project maintains three non-negotiable boundaries:
1. **Empirical Grounding:** Distinguishes verified astronomical observations from hypothetical constructs.
2. **Mathematical Formalism:** Translates every habitability assertion into testable probability distributions and differential equations.
3. **Engineering Reproducibility:** Delivers functional, open-source computational pipelines and documented models capable of independent execution and peer review.

### C. Scientific Feasibility Assessment
- **Observational Accessibility:** Exoplanets within $\approx 1\text{ kpc}$ are directly accessible to transmission spectroscopy (JWST, ELT). Extragalactic planets (extragalactic microlensing in M31) represent statistical populations. Extragalactic life detection is constrained to aggregated technosignatures or cosmological macro-engineering indicators.
- **Computational Tractability:** Simulating full biospheres atom-by-atom is mathematically intractable. Consequently, the programme employs **hierarchical coarse-graining**: general relativistic background $\rightarrow$ galactic metallicity and star-formation rates $\rightarrow$ stellar irradiance and flare statistics $\rightarrow$ 1D/3D planetary climate equilibrium $\rightarrow$ prebiotic reaction network topologies.

2. What is the minimum chemical network connectivity required to achieve autonomous catalytic closure and metabolic self-maintenance?

4. How do high-energy flaring environments of M-dwarf stars impact atmospheric water retention over gigayear timescales?
5. What formal Bayesian priors should govern the search for extraterrestrial intelligence given zero verified detections to date?
