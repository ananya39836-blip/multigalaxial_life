### 2. Documented Physical Approximations & Modeling Limitations

1. **Fluid Dynamics:**
   - Planetary atmospheres and oceans are modeled using simplified velocity vector fields and Stokes drag approximations ($F_{drag} = \frac{1}{2} C_d \rho A v^2$) rather than full Navier-Stokes DNS (Direct Numerical Simulation).
   - Micro-turbulence and thermal plume eddies are parameterized via stochastic noise fields.

2. **Kinematic & Contact Physics:**
   - Multi-body collision detection utilizes axis-aligned bounding boxes (AABB) and raycast terrain penetration tests rather than continuous non-convex mesh-to-mesh GJK solvers.
   - Ground reaction forces are resolved via spring-damper penalty formulations with velocity damping to ensure real-time $50\,\text{Hz}$ execution.

3. **Bioenergetics & Metabolic Scaling:**
   - Organism energy budgets simulate first-order thermodynamic bookkeeping (intake minus expenditure). Cellular respiration, ATP synthesis, and enzymatic kinetics are not modeled at molecular quantum levels.
   - Somatic damage is represented by a scalar health metric $H \in [0, 1]$ rather than histological tissue failure.

4. **Sensor Simulation:**
   - Virtual cameras utilize pinhole perspective projection matrices and rasterized depth buffers; diffraction limits, quantum efficiency curves, and atmospheric scintillation are modeled via empirical Gaussian noise rather than full physical wavefront optics.
   - Chemical sensors perform point-sampling on interpolated 2D scalar fields rather than physical molecular binding kinetics.

5. **Virtual Microprocessor Simulation:**
   - Simulated clock cycles, memory word constraints, and instruction queue latencies represent an abstract algorithmic constraint model rather than cycle-accurate SPICE or VHDL semiconductor emulation.
